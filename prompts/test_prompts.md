# VoxMea - Test Prompts

These prompts are used by test_generation.py to evaluate style replication fidelity.

## 1. Same Topic as Control (Direct Comparison)

Write a paragraph about [CONTROL_TOPIC]. Make it personal and reflective, written in your own voice.

## 2. New Topic — Similar Register

Write about a recent experience that made you stop and think. It could be something you observed, a conversation you had, or a moment that stood out.

## 3. Opinion / Argumentative

What is something you believe strongly about that most people seem to disagree with? Explain your perspective.

## 4. Informal / Casual

Tell me about your day. What happened? What are you thinking about? Just write whatever comes to mind.

## 5. Short-Form (1-2 sentences)

Explain in one or two sentences why [CONTROL_TOPIC] matters to you.

## 6. Long-Form Reflection (500+ words)

Describe a place that shaped who you are. It could be a childhood home, a city you lived in, a room, or even a state of mind. Go deep — what made it special, what did you learn there, how did it change you?

## 7. Technical / Analytical

Explain a concept in your field of expertise to someone who is curious but unfamiliar with it. Avoid jargon where possible, but don't dumb it down — teach them.

## 8. Emotional / Personal

Write a letter to your younger self. What would you want them to know? What would you warn them about? What would you reassure them about?

## 9. Descriptive / Sensory

Describe your current environment. What do you see, hear, smell, and feel right now? Paint a picture with words.

## 10. Abstract / Philosophical

What is something you've changed your mind about in the last five years? How did your perspective shift, and what prompted the change?

---

*Note: [CONTROL_TOPIC] and [CONTROL_TOPIC_DESCRIPTION] are replaced at runtime based on the control text analysis.*
